## Why this problem is unresolved

The live litter-mat, automatic-litter-box, and enzyme-cleaner guides all returned HTTP 200 on 2026-08-24. The litter-mat guide still names only legacy Gorilla Grip ASIN `B01MS6PLM6`. The 2026-08-06 catalog review marked that active record `retire-review` because its Amazon page returned Page Not Found and recommended evaluating a current exact Gorilla Grip child or another low-profile mat with curl, trip, floor, and cleaning limits.

The reader problem is not "buy a litter mat." It is preserving a usable exit path in a small bathroom, laundry nook, or hallway. A mat is a poor fit if the cat jumps around it, a door catches it, an edge curls, the backing moves on the floor, or the cleanup method is harder than vacuuming the loose litter directly. This run therefore weighted exact footprint, edge behavior, surface and liquid-cleaning burden, current first-party documentation, and owner failure patterns above commercial value.

## Evidence limits and labels

- **Verified specification** means the exact current Amazon child or a current first-party page exposed the stated identity or instruction.
- **Manufacturer claim** means a brand or current brand owner stated it; this run did not independently test it.
- **Independent review** means a third party evaluated or summarized the product outside the manufacturer listing.
- **Owner-reported pattern** means multiple review themes or an aggregate review summary; it is not a controlled test and does not establish a universal result.
- **Editorial inference** means the source evidence was applied to the apartment exit-path problem.
- Amazon pages were readable through a text proxy during this run and exposed exact child titles and featured Add to Cart states. No price or availability statement is approved for publication, and every pending candidate needs Tuesday re-verification.
- The official CPSC recall CSV checked on 2026-08-24 was last modified 2026-08-21. No exact evaluated litter-mat model or ASIN matched. The dataset contained a 2020 Gorilla Commerce oven-liner recall, which is unrelated to the evaluated litter mat. A no-match is a dated database check, not safety certification.
