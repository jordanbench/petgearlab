## Why this problem is unresolved

The live enzyme-cleaner guide, new-puppy setup guide, and guide hub each returned HTTP 200 on 2026-08-31. The enzyme-cleaner guide does not contain an enzyme-cleaner candidate: its live table exposes only ASINs `B01MS6PLM6`, `B0002AR0II`, `B09DSVFNRW`, and `B07G642RMQ` for a litter mat, dog toy, fountain, and car-seat cover. It therefore does not help a reader choose a formula, surface routine, dwell time, extraction step, or pet re-entry limit. The new-puppy setup guide also names cleaning as a decision criterion without providing an evidence-backed cleanup product.

This run therefore did not ask which cleaner has the largest bottle, strongest odor promise, or best commission. It asked whether an exact current variant can support a practical fresh-urine routine: blot or extract the liquid first, test colorfastness, reach the full affected depth, preserve the documented dwell time, blot or extract again, ventilate, and keep pets and children away until the area is dry. A cleaner cannot resolve repeated urination caused by medical, litter-box, training, stress, or access problems.

- **Verified specification** means the exact current Amazon child exposed the product identity, size, pack count, scent, or item model.
- **Manufacturer claim or instruction** means the brand stated a formula property, surface scope, dwell time, or use limit; this run did not independently test it.
- **Independent review** means a third party tested or evaluated the exact product outside the manufacturer and Amazon listing.
- **Owner-reported pattern** means multiple retailer reviews or an aggregate theme; it is anecdotal, can be incentive-biased, and does not establish universal results.
- **Editorial inference** means the evidence was applied to the fresh-urine carpet or upholstery problem.
- Amazon pages were readable through a text proxy on 2026-08-31 and exposed exact child titles and item details. No price or availability statement is approved for publication, and every pending candidate needs Tuesday exact-listing re-verification.
